Cleaner Guru or Fast Cleaner?

Compare the workflow and the purchase terms before choosing. Cleaner Guru advertises media, contacts, and email cleanup. Fast Cleaner is worth exploring for photo and video review with listed monthly and lifetime purchases. Apple Photos can be enough if your only job is merging duplicates it identifies.

The useful comparison is the task you can finish, the files you control, and the amount you agree to pay. Neither an “AI” label nor a high rating establishes which app will select your preferred photo.

Compare the billing interval, not just the price

Fast Cleaner’s US catalog lists $4.99 monthly and $14.99 Lifetime Pro Access. Check what each purchase unlocks and the offer on your own checkout screen. A free download is not proof that all cleanup is free.

Cleaner Guru’s billing terms make renewal depend on the period selected at purchase. Generic Premium and Unlimited Access catalog names do not establish an interval; read the actual offer rather than treating one displayed amount as a universal weekly price.

For a one-off cleanup, first see whether the available free tools solve your task. For regular use, compare the expected cost over the same period and the features included. Verify whether a trial renews, the renewal amount, and when it ends.

Is Cleaner Guru safe?

Published disclosures help you assess privacy; they do not certify security. Cleaner Guru’s policy describes collecting device and interaction information and sharing some data for analytics and advertising. This describes other data flows; it does not by itself prove that photo files are uploaded.

Read media processing, analytics, and permissions separately. A claim that analysis happens locally does not establish that an app has no network activity. Ask which feature requires each permission, especially if you only intend to review photos.

Our privacy policy describes local media analysis and separate limited analytics. A retrieved Fast Cleaner store label said “Data Not Collected,” which needs clarification against the policy for the relevant version. We do not use that label to claim zero data collection or a security advantage.

Try a small review before a large cleanup

Use copies of a few photos you can afford to experiment with, including exact copies, different expressions, and edited versions. Inspect each suggested group and check whether you can change the selection before removal.

  1. Confirm which photos the app is allowed to read.
  2. Compare faces, text, crops, and edits before accepting a suggested choice.
  3. Read the removal confirmation and keep an independent backup of important files.
  4. Check the same device-storage screen afterward; an item count is not a byte measurement.

We have not run this comparison, so there is no supported winner for scan speed, false matches, or recovered storage. Use the same source files and device if you compare those yourself.

Common questions

Is Cleaner Guru free?

It is listed as free to download with in-app purchases. Verify usable free actions and the selected trial or subscription in the app.

Is Fast Cleaner better than Cleaner Guru?

This desk comparison cannot establish a performance winner. Choose from the task, current terms, and review controls, or compare both using the same backed-up selection.

Does deleting Cleaner Guru cancel the subscription?

Its billing terms say deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Use the cancellation route for the provider that billed you.

Can either app guarantee safe photo choices?

No tool knows which expression, edit, or original you value. Inspect suggestions and preserve important files separately.
