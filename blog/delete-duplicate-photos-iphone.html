Quick answer

In current Photos layouts, open Collections → Utilities → Duplicates. Compare the items and use Merge for groups you want to combine. Older Photos layouts place Utilities under Albums. Apple introduced duplicate detection in iOS 16.

Before merging or deleting

Preserve a separate, verified copy of irreplaceable pictures. Read Apple’s iCloud deletion and recovery guidance: a change in a synced library can affect other devices, and recovery has exceptions.

Look at faces, dates, crops, edits, resolution, and meaningful details. Two shots taken a moment apart may each be worth keeping. A visually similar image is a reason to compare, not an instruction to delete.

Use Photos to merge identified duplicates

  1. Open Photos and find Collections → Utilities → Duplicates, or Utilities under Albums in an older layout.
  2. Inspect a group and open images for a closer comparison.
  3. Tap Merge for the group you want to combine and read the confirmation.
  4. Check the remaining image and its details before proceeding to more groups.

Follow Apple’s merging instructions for your software version. Start with a small batch so you understand the result before making a larger change.

If Duplicates does not appear

Apple’s detection guidance says the phone must be locked and connected to power. Indexing and detection may take a few days, depending on the library and background tasks. No identified duplicates also means no Duplicates collection.

Prevent copies you do not need

  • Review saved media and automatic photo-saving options in the messaging apps you use; controls vary by app and version.
  • After a burst or rapid sequence, choose favorites while the difference between shots is still clear.
  • Check whether an editing or export workflow creates a separate file before importing it again.
  • Review new clutter periodically instead of relying on a fixed cleanup schedule or expected saving.

Finish by checking Settings → General → iPhone Storage. If the warning remains, follow storage troubleshooting after deleting photos rather than deleting more pictures blindly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does iPhone have a built-in duplicate finder?

Yes. Starting with iOS 16, Photos can identify duplicates and offer merging. In current layouts, look under Collections → Utilities → Duplicates; older layouts place Utilities under Albums.

Why is the Duplicates collection missing?

Photos needs to index the library and identify duplicates. Apple says detection requires a locked iPhone connected to power and can take a few days. If no duplicates have been identified, the collection does not appear.

Will deletion affect iCloud Photos?

Yes, when iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting media can remove it from your other synced devices. Preserve important files separately before cleanup.

Can I always recover a removed photo for 30 days?

Do not rely on that guarantee. Recently Deleted normally provides a 30-day recovery window, but permanent deletion removes that option. Apple also documents an exception when your iCloud storage limit has been exceeded.

How much space will removing duplicates save?

It depends on the files, their sizes, and which copies are stored locally. Review the selection and compare device storage before and after; an item count is not a storage measurement.
