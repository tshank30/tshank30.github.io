Two Ways to Bulk Delete Screenshots on iPhone

Compare the manual method vs. the smart approach:

📱 Manual Method (Photos App)

  1. Open Photos app
  2. Go to Albums → Screenshots
  3. Tap Select in top right
  4. Tap Select All or manually select screenshots
  5. Tap the trash icon to delete
⚠️ Problem: This deletes ALL screenshots, including ones you want to keep. No way to identify duplicates or review by type.

⚡ Smart Method (Fast Cleaner)

  1. Open Fast Cleaner
  2. Tap Junk Detection
  3. AI automatically groups screenshots
  4. Review before deleting — keep important ones
  5. Tap Clean to bulk delete selected
✅ Benefit: Groups duplicate screenshots, lets you review before deletion, and identifies other junk like web images and blurry photos too.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete all screenshots on iPhone at once?

Go to Photos > Albums > Screenshots, tap Select, then Select All, and tap the trash icon. This deletes everything in the Screenshots album. For a smarter approach, use Fast Cleaner which lets you review screenshots and groups duplicates before bulk deletion.

How do I bulk delete screenshots on iPhone?

You can bulk delete through Photos app or use Fast Cleaner. The Photos method (Albums > Screenshots > Select All > Delete) removes ALL screenshots. Fast Cleaner's AI groups similar screenshots and lets you selectively bulk delete while keeping important ones.

Why do I have so many screenshots on my iPhone?

Screenshots accumulate from everyday activities: saving receipts, capturing addresses, sharing conversations, noting prices, social media posts, and accidental screenshots. Most people have 500+ screenshots they've forgotten about, often consuming 1-3GB of storage.

Do screenshots take up a lot of storage on iPhone?

Yes, screenshots can consume 1-3GB or more. Each screenshot is typically 200KB-2MB depending on content. With hundreds or thousands of screenshots accumulated over time, they become a significant storage drain. Duplicate screenshots of the same content waste even more.

Can I recover deleted screenshots?

Yes, for 30 days. When you delete screenshots (whether through Photos app or Fast Cleaner), they go to the "Recently Deleted" album. You have 30 days to recover any you deleted by mistake before they're permanently removed.

Does Fast Cleaner find duplicate screenshots?

Yes, Fast Cleaner's AI groups duplicate and similar screenshots together. If you screenshotted the same message conversation multiple times or took multiple screenshots of the same receipt, the app groups them so you can keep just one version.
