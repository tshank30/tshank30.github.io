Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress videos on iPhone without losing quality?

Use Fast Cleaner's video compression feature. Download the app, select "Compress Videos," choose your videos, and pick Medium compression for the best balance of size reduction and quality. Modern compression can reduce files by 50-80% with virtually no visible difference on phones and tablets.

Does iPhone have a built-in video compressor?

No, iPhone doesn't have a built-in way to compress existing videos. You can only change recording quality for future videos (Settings > Camera > Record Video). To compress videos already on your phone, you need an app like Fast Cleaner.

How much space do iPhone videos take up?

It depends on recording quality. 1080p at 30fps uses ~60MB per minute. 4K at 60fps uses ~400MB per minute. A 5-minute 4K video can consume 2GB. If you record often at high quality, videos can easily take up 20-50GB of your storage.

Will compressing videos make them look bad?

Not at recommended settings. Medium compression produces videos that look virtually identical to originals when viewed on phones, tablets, or most TVs. Quality loss is only noticeable when zooming in significantly or viewing on large 4K displays up close.

How long does video compression take?

About 10-30 seconds per minute of video, depending on your iPhone model. Newer iPhones compress faster. You can queue multiple videos and let them process while you do other things — compression runs in the background.

Can I recover the original video after compression?

Compression replaces the original, so back up important videos first. However, if you use iCloud Photos, the original may still be in iCloud for a while. For truly irreplaceable videos, consider keeping originals on a computer or external drive.
