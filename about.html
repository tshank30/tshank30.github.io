About Fast Cleaner

Privacy-first storage cleanup for Android and iPhone.

🔒 Privacy First

Fast Cleaner is a privacy-first storage cleaner. Core duplicate and junk-detection flows are designed to run on your device, so your personal media does not need to be uploaded to our servers. Learn more about our privacy →

👨‍💻 Developer Info

Published by independent developers committed to building privacy-respecting apps. We believe you shouldn't have to sacrifice privacy for functionality. Questions? Reach us at tshank30@gmail.com.

📊 What We Collect

No photo uploads. Limited analytics only. Junk detection and classification happen on-device, and we collect only screen event analytics and related diagnostics to improve the app experience. Your photos and classification results are never uploaded to our servers. See our full privacy policy →

📲 Available on Both Stores

Fast Cleaner is available on the App Store and Google Play. Users typically recover 5-15GB of storage on their first cleanup.
