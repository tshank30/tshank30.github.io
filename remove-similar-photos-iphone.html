Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove similar photos on iPhone?

Use Fast Cleaner's AI-powered scan. Download the app, tap "Find Duplicates," and the AI will group similar photos together. Unlike iPhone's built-in feature which only finds exact copies, Fast Cleaner detects visually similar images — burst shots, multiple angles, and near-identical photos.

Can iPhone detect similar photos that aren't exact duplicates?

No, iPhone's Duplicates album only finds exact pixel-perfect copies. It won't detect similar photos like 10 shots of the same sunset, burst photos, or nearly-identical images. For these, you need an AI-powered app like Fast Cleaner that uses perceptual hashing to compare visual content.

What's the difference between duplicate and similar photos?

Duplicates are exact copies; similar photos are different shots that look nearly identical. For example, if you save the same photo twice, that's a duplicate. If you take 10 photos of the same scene with tiny differences between them, those are similar photos. Similar photos typically waste more storage.

How does Fast Cleaner's AI find similar photos?

Fast Cleaner uses on-device AI with perceptual hashing. It analyzes the visual content of each photo — colors, shapes, composition — to identify images that look similar even if they're not pixel-perfect matches. All processing happens locally on your iPhone for complete privacy.

Will removing similar photos delete my originals?

You choose which versions to keep. Fast Cleaner groups similar photos and auto-selects lower quality versions, but you have full control. Review each group before cleaning. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can recover any mistakes.
