How On-Device AI Works

Fast Cleaner’s core media analysis runs locally, according to our privacy policy. Local analysis and analytics are separate data flows.

Can photo AI run without uploading media?

Apple’s Core ML documentation says running a model strictly on a person’s device removes any need for a network connection. This supports the technical possibility of local photo analysis; an app’s implementation determines its actual data flows.

Source: Apple — Core ML. Checked November 21, 2026.

Can Android photo analysis run on the phone?

Yes. Google says ML Kit’s processing happens on-device. Its documentation covers Android and iOS and describes offline image processing. Local machine learning is available on both platforms; this does not establish which framework a particular cleaner uses.

Source: Google — ML Kit. Checked November 21, 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fast Cleaner available for Android, and is it free?

Yes. Fast Cleaner for Android is free and is listed on Google Play as Duplicate Photo Cleaner AI by CrocApps. Its listing describes on-device processing and review before deletion. iPhone has a separate pricing model.

Does Fast Cleaner upload my photos to a server?

No. Fast Cleaner is designed around on-device cleanup flows, so duplicate and junk detection does not require uploading your personal media for server-side analysis.

How can the app use AI without uploading photos?

AI models can run locally on a phone. Fast Cleaner’s privacy policy describes on-device junk detection and classification. Using AI does not inherently require uploading media to a provider’s servers.

What data does Fast Cleaner collect?

Limited analytics only. We collect screen event analytics and related diagnostics to improve the app experience. We do not upload your photos, videos, contacts, or on-device junk classification results to our servers. See our privacy policy for complete details.

Is Fast Cleaner safe to use with sensitive photos?

On-device analysis reduces sharing with the cleaner provider. It does not guarantee complete security. Review photo permissions, protect your device, and check your photo service’s backup, synced-deletion, and recovery settings before removing sensitive files.

Do I need an internet connection to use Fast Cleaner?

Core cleanup flows are designed to work offline. You can scan for duplicates, review results, and clean your library without depending on a constant internet connection.

Why should I trust Fast Cleaner over other photo cleaners?

Transparency and architecture. Fast Cleaner is designed around local cleanup analysis, clear review steps, and a privacy policy that explains the limited analytics used to improve the app experience.
