How On-Device AI Works

Fast Cleaner uses Apple's Core ML to run AI models directly on your iPhone — no cloud needed.

🧠 Neural Engine Processing

Modern iPhones have dedicated neural processing hardware. Fast Cleaner uses this to analyze photos locally, comparing images using perceptual hashing without any network requests.

📱 Everything Stays Local

Your photos are analyzed in iPhone's memory, results are stored locally, and cleanup happens entirely on-device. The only network activity is checking for app updates.

🔒 No Account Required

You don't need to create an account or sign in. This means no user profiles, no photo libraries associated with your identity, and no way for anyone to access your content.

✈️ Works Offline

Because everything is on-device, Fast Cleaner works perfectly in airplane mode. Scan your library, find duplicates, and clean up without any internet connection.

Our Privacy Commitment

Fast Cleaner is built by developers who care about privacy. We designed the app this way because:

  • We don't want your data. Running servers to store millions of users' photos would be expensive and create liability. By processing everything on-device, we avoid this entirely.
  • We believe private apps are better apps. When you know your photos stay private, you can clean confidently without worrying about who might see your personal moments.
  • Trust is everything. One privacy breach would destroy our reputation. On-device processing means there's nothing to breach in the first place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fast Cleaner upload my photos to a server?

No, absolutely not. Fast Cleaner processes everything 100% on your iPhone using on-device AI. Your photos, videos, and personal data never leave your device. We don't have servers that store user content because we never see your photos in the first place.

How can the app use AI without uploading photos?

Fast Cleaner uses Apple's Core ML framework to run AI models directly on your iPhone's processor. The neural engine in modern iPhones is powerful enough to analyze photos locally. This means you get smart duplicate detection without sacrificing privacy.

What data does Fast Cleaner collect?

Minimal anonymous analytics only. We collect basic app crash reports and feature usage counts to improve the app. We never collect, store, or transmit your photos, contacts, or personal information. See our privacy policy for complete details.

Is Fast Cleaner safe to use with sensitive photos?

Yes. Since all processing happens on your device and no photos are uploaded, your sensitive photos remain completely private. The app only accesses photos you explicitly select, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted where you can recover them for 30 days.

Do I need an internet connection to use Fast Cleaner?

No, Fast Cleaner works completely offline. Because all processing happens on your device, you can scan for duplicates, review results, and clean your library without any internet connection. The only network activity is optional (checking for app updates).

Why should I trust Fast Cleaner over other photo cleaners?

Transparency and architecture. Many photo cleaners require cloud processing and have vague privacy policies. Fast Cleaner is architecturally designed to be private — there's no server infrastructure to receive your photos even if we wanted to. You can verify this by using the app in airplane mode.
