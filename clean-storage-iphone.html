Why Is My iPhone Storage Full?

Understanding what's consuming your storage is the first step. Here are the usual culprits:

📷

Photos & Videos (40-60%)

By far the biggest storage consumer. Duplicates, similar shots, 4K videos, and screenshots pile up faster than you realize.

📱

Apps & App Data (15-25%)

Apps accumulate caches over time. TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, and games can grow to gigabytes of cached data.

⚙️

System Data (5-15%)

Safari cache, Siri voices, logs, and temporary files. This mysterious category can grow to 10-20GB on older iPhones.

💬

Messages (5-15%)

Years of text messages with photos, videos, and attachments add up. A single conversation can contain gigabytes of media.
