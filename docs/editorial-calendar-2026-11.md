## Why this order

Begin with the direct storage and duplicate-photo problems, then explain device/cloud capacity and backup actions. Follow with similar-photo judgment and large-video review, then compression quality, permissions, metadata, and a broader Android workflow. Each article links to guides already present on `main`, so it does not depend on another scheduled PR going live first.

## Publication routine

- Before release: use that article’s publication brief; recheck platform-specific steps and the actual byline date.
- On release: merge the single PR, verify the Pages deployment and live URL, check canonical and sitemap, then inspect the URL in Search Console. Request indexing once if appropriate; do not repeatedly submit an unchanged page.
- After release: share where it is relevant and review the page’s query data after 7 and 28 days. Keep a record of impressions, clicks, queries, and any app-link engagement you actually measure.
- Improve an existing guide where the intent overlaps. Do not create multiple near-identical pages for the same query.

## Google guidance

[Publication-date guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/publication-dates) recommends dates that reflect actual publication and consistent visible and structured dates. [Indexing requests](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl) do not guarantee inclusion. The planned dates here become actual dates only when the articles are released.
